# Aigenchain Glossary

## ADR

Architecture Decision Record.

A document that permanently records important architectural decisions and their rationale.

---

## AigenRuntime

The future native runtime responsible for execution, orchestration, scheduling, and lifecycle management within Aigenchain.

---

## Offline-First

A design philosophy where core functionality remains operational without internet connectivity whenever technically feasible.

---

## Local AI

Artificial intelligence models executed on local hardware under the user's control.

---

## Data Sovereignty

The principle that users retain complete ownership and control over their data.

---

## Orchestrator

A subsystem responsible for coordinating multiple services, tasks, and execution flows.

---

## Scheduler

The component responsible for planning and managing execution timing and resource allocation.

---

## Memory Engine

The subsystem responsible for persistent and contextual information storage and retrieval.

---

## Compatibility Layer

A component that enables interoperability with third-party technologies without making them architectural dependencies.

---

## Autonomous Digital Factory

The long-term vision of Aigenchain in which the platform can plan, create, test, optimize, and maintain commercially valuable digital products with minimal human intervention.
